## Explanation

1. **The `COINBASE_MATURITY = 100` consensus rule**: Newly created bitcoins (coinbase transactions generated by miners as block rewards and transaction fees) cannot be spent until 100 additional blocks have been mined on top of the block containing them (reaching 100 confirmations). This rule protects network stability: if a short chain reorganization occurs near the tip, unconfirmed coinbase transactions in reorged blocks would become completely invalid (since those block hashes and coinbase outputs no longer exist in the active chain), causing cascading transaction invalidations across the network.
2. **Why 101 blocks are mined on a fresh chain**: On a newly initialized regtest chain (height 0), Block 1 creates the miner's first 50 BTC coinbase output. At height 1, this output has 1 confirmation and is marked `immature`. Mining 100 additional blocks advances the tip to height 101. At height 101, Block 1 has accumulated 100 confirmations ($101 - 1 + 1 = 101$ blocks depth), making its 50 BTC reward spendable in `trusted` balance, while blocks 2 through 101 remain immature.

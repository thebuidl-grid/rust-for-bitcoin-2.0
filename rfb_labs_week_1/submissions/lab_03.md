## Explanation

- **`COINBASE_MATURITY = 100` Rule**: Bitcoin consensus rules require coinbase outputs (block reward transactions generated by miners) to be locked for at least 100 confirmations before they can be spent.
- **Why mine 101 blocks**: When the first block is mined, the height becomes 1. The coinbase reward from this first block becomes mature and spendable only after 100 additional blocks are mined on top of it. Thus, the chain must reach a height of at least `1 + 100 = 101` blocks before the first coinbase payout is spendable. This rule protects users from chain reorganizations; if a chain split occurs and mines a different tip, a recently generated block may be orphaned, causing its coinbase reward to vanish. The 100-block buffer ensures the coinbase is deeply buried in history before being spent.

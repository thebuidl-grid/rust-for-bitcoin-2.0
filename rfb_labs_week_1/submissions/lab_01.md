## Explanation

Polar defines and operates the local network topology. Docker runs the isolated node
process generated by Polar. Bitcoin Core is the actual validating node and RPC server;
its `getblockchaininfo` response is the authority for the selected chain and tip.
Regtest is Bitcoin Core's private regression-test chain: blocks can be generated on
demand, coins have no real-world value, and this isolated genesis hash is distinct from
mainnet and testnet. The observed `chain: regtest`, height 0, and genesis best-block hash
therefore prove the intended safe environment was active.
